# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Playa a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported through the repository's private security or maintainer contact mechanisms. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior violates this code.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
